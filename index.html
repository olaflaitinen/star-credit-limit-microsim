Кредитный лимит: кто проиграет?

Credit limit: who loses? Team STAR

A household-level microsimulation of tightening Kazakhstan's consumer-credit debt-service limit (КДН, now 50% of income) on the synthetic D-004 household survey, 2023-2024. A flat 30% limit hits the poorest households hardest; a targeted limit gives the regulator the same cut in consumer credit without blocking more loans for food and medicine.

СимуляторSet the limit and see who loses access to credit, by income fifth, region and loan purpose. Презентация финала18 slides with the 7-minute speech in the speaker notes. Press N to hide the notes. Код и данныеPython pipeline, results, findings and method notes on GitHub.

Synthetic data: the directions are reliable, the magnitudes are to be confirmed on real data. All numbers and caveats: docs/FINDINGS.md.
