# Presenting on Zoom with speaker notes

The speech is in the speaker notes of each of the 18 slides (the `<aside>` at the end of every slide file). Each note starts with a tag such as `[Спикер 1 · 0:45-1:10]`. Speaker 1 has slides 1 to 9 (to 3:20), Speaker 2 has slides 10 to 18 (to 6:50).

## What the audience sees

- **Sharing one window:** if the notes are visible in that browser window, the audience sees them. Presenting the deck full screen hides the notes for them, and for you.
- **Sharing the entire screen:** they see everything on that screen. Zoom shares one monitor at a time, so with two monitors you share the one with the slides and keep the notes on the other.

## One-laptop setup

1. Present the deck full screen and share that browser window in Zoom.
2. Read the speech from a second device: a phone, a tablet or a teammate's laptop (use `STAR_speech_7min.txt`, or open the same deck link there and read the notes).

Safest alternative: download the deck as PowerPoint and use Presenter View (notes on your screen, slides on the shared screen). Check after downloading that the notes came along.

## Simulator during the talk

Open the simulator in a second tab of the same browser window before the call. When a browser window is shared, Zoom shows its active tab, so switching tabs is enough. If it opens in a separate window, use Zoom's "New Share" to switch.

Slide 15's note has the demo script (35 seconds: click "Единый лимит 30%", then "Адресный лимит", then "Адресный + соцзащита") and a fallback: if the tab won't open, say «Покажем симулятор после выступления» and move on.

## Caveat

Anyone who has the deck link can open the notes. Sending the PowerPoint file has the same issue unless the notes are deleted from that copy.
