Form D-004: reading the tables

Notes on kv_vopr12, NOMVOPR and the GR columns

Answers to the questions that came in about kv_vopr12. Counts are from the synthetic files you have, so they reproduce.

The form

Quarterly household budget survey. Twelve tables per quarter: kv_vopr0-kv_vopr7, kv_vopr9-kv_vopr12, there is no kv_vopr8. They are at d004/<year>/<quarter>/<table>.csv. All join on NOMER, the household code. kv_vopr0 has one row per household, the rest have zero or more.

NOMVOPR is the question number inside a table

Most tables hold several questions. The number is the table’s own number plus the question’s index within it.

kv_vopr0, kv_vopr1 header, non-food goods no such column
kv_vopr2 utilities 21
kv_vopr3 communications 31
kv_vopr4 education 41
kv_vopr5 health 51
kv_vopr6 personal services, leisure, other financial 60, 61, 62
kv_vopr7 transport 71
kv_vopr9 production, section 4 91-99, 913, 914
kv_vopr10 production, section 4 101, 102
kv_vopr11 income, section 3 111
kv_vopr12 borrowed funds, section 2 200, 1211, 1212

The number is unique only inside its own table. 41 is the only value in kv_vopr4 and also one of kv_vopr9’s questions.

GR1, GR2 … are column numbers on the paper form

“Графа” means a column of the printed questionnaire. The field name says where on the page the value came from, not what it measures, so the same name means different things in different tables: in kv_vopr9 GR1 is cost, in kv_vopr11 GR1-GR23 are 23 income types “in form order”. To decode a GRn, open that table’s page in the questionnaire.

kv_vopr12: three kinds of row

2021-2023 have 9 columns; 2024 adds NOMVOPR, NOMOTV and GR1.

NOMVOPR NOMVNEW the row is filled
200 20 one of six borrowed-funds items KODNU, STOIMK
1211 21 answer to question 2.1, purpose KOD
1212 22 answer to question 2.2, source KOD

KOD and KODNU never both appear. Rows 1211 and 1212 carry no amount. KODNU’s six items are decoded in documentation/d004/2023/коды/kv_vopr12_Заемные средства.xls.

KOD holds answers to two different questions

That is the whole answer to “why 9 codes”. The column pools two questions, each with nine options, and the ninth is “Other” in both.

sort(table(purpose$name_ru), decreasing = TRUE)

Checked on all sixteen files: nothing is left unmatched.

NOMOTV and GR1 carry nothing here

NOMOTV (“answer number”) is a function of NOMVOPR: always 1 on rows 1211 and 1212, 0 or blank on rows 200. It is filled on 57% of 2024 rows, and whether it is blank depends on the region: 0% blank in TE 61, about 70% in TE 10 and 55. That is data entry, not content. Use NOMVOPR instead.

GR1 has exactly one distinct value in all of 2024 (zero) and is blank in over half the rows. It does not exist in 2021-2023 and is not in the 2024 structure document. On the paper form columns 1-3 of this section are the three months of the quarter and column 4 is the quarterly total; only the total was kept, and it is in STOIMK. Ignore GR1 in this table.

Two traps

kv_vopr9 and kv_vopr10 switch numbering schemes between Q3 and Q4 of 2023. 2021 uses 91-99 and 101, 102; 2022 through 2023 Q3 uses 41-481 and 412, 413; from 2023 Q4 it reverts. Map them before any cross-year work:

kv_vopr9 :  91=41   92=42   93=43   94=44   95=451  96=45
            97=46   98=47   99=481  913=410 914=411
kv_vopr10:  101=412  102=413

Note 95=451 and 96=45: not in order, so “swap the 9 for a 4” gets those two wrong.

The 2024 documentation is buried. 2021-2023 are laid out flat, but documentation/d004/2024/ holds four nested folders named d004_1kv2024parol2025/D004_1kv2024/, and there is no questionnaire for 2024 at all, which is awkward, since NOMVOPR, NOMOTV and GR1 only appear that year. The decodings are identical across years, so use the 2023 documents.
